# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mac users who write across many applications and want voice dictation to feel immediate, dependable, and private. They use Onyx Scribe in the middle of another task, so its controls and feedback must be understandable at a glance without stealing focus.

## Product Purpose

Onyx Scribe turns speech into polished text locally, inserts it at the cursor, and gives users control over language, tone, vocabulary, and cleanup. Success means users can dictate confidently throughout macOS, understand what the app is doing in every moment, and trust that their words and history remain on their Mac.

## Brand Personality

Premium, private, intelligent, calm, and quietly distinctive. The voice is concise and reassuring, with confidence coming from precise behavior rather than technical spectacle.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI dashboard, neon gaming overlay, decorative glassmorphism demo, or a collection of identical rounded cards. Avoid gratuitous gradients, excessive glow, unfamiliar replacements for standard macOS controls, and motion that competes with the user's active application.

## Design Principles

- Stay out of the user's way: Onyx Scribe appears only when it has useful state or action to communicate.
- Make system state unmistakable: recording, processing, success, degraded output, and failure must read instantly through more than color alone.
- Earn trust through clarity: local processing, permissions, engine readiness, and privacy behavior should never be ambiguous.
- Use brand materials with restraint: onyx depth and blackberry-violet light identify the product, while hierarchy and content do the real work.
- Behave like a good Mac citizen: preserve familiar controls, keyboard access, focus behavior, and non-activating floating surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA text contrast where applicable, visible keyboard focus, screen-reader labels for icon-only controls, and non-color state indicators. Respect the macOS Reduce Motion setting and keep essential audio-level feedback understandable without continuous decorative animation.
