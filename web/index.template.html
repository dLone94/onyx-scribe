How it works

One gesture. Then get out of the way.

The whole interaction is a key-hold. Everything else — transcription, filler removal, punctuation, tone — happens in the second it takes to release.

1

Hold the key

Press and hold Right ⌥ — or any hotkey you set. A calm pill appears with a live waveform.

2

Speak naturally

Say it how you'd say it — "um"s, restarts and all. Watch the words appear live in the pill as you talk.

3

Release

Let go. Cleaned, formatted text is pasted straight into whatever you were typing in — no window to copy from.
